local_fire_department Flagship Experience 1

THERMALX — 2D Numerical Thermal Field Simulator

Simulates the 2D thermodynamic temperature field of a laptop chassis containing realistic heat sources: CPU Core Die (85°C), Discrete GPU (75°C), and Battery Pack (40°C) with active fan cooling.

Governing Equation: \( k \nabla^2 T(x,y) + Q(x,y) - h_{\text{conv}}(T - T_{\text{amb}}) = 0 \)
  • Jacobi Method: Computes the entire grid using previous step values; standard baseline.
  • Gauss-Seidel Method: Immediately reuses updated cell values in adjacent calculations, producing a 2x to 3x convergence speedup.
  • Bisection Cooling Optimizer: Automatically finds the exact cooling fan percentage needed to stabilize the peak chassis temperature strictly under 75°C.
  • FLIR Infrared vs Raw Mesh: One-click toggle between continuous thermal camera smoothing and discrete finite-difference cells.

5. Examiner Viva Cheat Sheet (Top Questions & Answers)

Q1: Why does Gauss-Seidel converge faster than Jacobi?

In Jacobi, step \( k+1 \) uses only values from step \( k \). In Gauss-Seidel, newly updated values are immediately fed into subsequent coordinate updates within the same iteration. This effectively squares the spectral radius of the iteration matrix: \( \rho(T_{GS}) \approx \rho(T_J)^2 \), making Gauss-Seidel roughly 2x faster.

Q2: What is the convergence rate of Bisection vs Newton-Raphson?

Bisection is linear (\( \varepsilon_{k+1} = 0.5 \varepsilon_k \)) and unconditionally guaranteed to converge by the Intermediate Value Theorem. Newton-Raphson is quadratic (\( \varepsilon_{k+1} \propto \varepsilon_k^2 \)), meaning the number of correct decimal digits doubles with each step.

Q3: How does the Power Method calculate PageRank?

The hyperlink network forms a column-stochastic transition matrix \( M \). Adding a damping factor (\( d = 0.85 \)) guarantees an irreducible Google matrix \( G \). By the Perron-Frobenius theorem, \( \lambda_1 = 1.000 \) is the unique dominant eigenvalue, and the Power Iteration Method isolates its principal eigenvector, which gives each web page's ranking.
